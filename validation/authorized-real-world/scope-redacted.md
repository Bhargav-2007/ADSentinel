# Scope, redacted

Only the personally controlled isolated host-only lab and explicit owner-listed hosts/domain are in scope. Source/target addresses and domain/account inventory are private. All physical networks, internet, production, education, company, ISP, provider and other virtual networks are out of scope.

Maximum run: 60 minutes; concurrency limit five (implementation runs sequentially). Stop on instability. Spraying, relay, unapproved extraction and object/configuration modifications require separate permission. Evidence stays local for 90 days under owner retention rules. No cloud endpoint is authorized by this scope.
